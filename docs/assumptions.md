# Assumptions and limitations

- This is an unofficial applicant-created prototype based on public information accessed on 2026-08-26.
- No access to Valuatum internal architecture, APIs, models, scoring rules, data contracts, customers, or commercial economics is claimed.
- Every runtime record and analytical output is synthetic.
- `observed` means the outcome is directly visible in public Valuatum material; it does not mean the proposed capability boundary matches Valuatum's internal architecture.
- `inferred` means the complete ability is reasonably implied by visible product behavior.
- `proposed` means introduced by this prototype.
- Pricing is illustrative and not a recommendation about existing Valuatum pricing.
- Authentication, billing, production persistence, real-data licensing, audit, and formal decision governance are intentionally out of scope.
